## Goal
Make impact claims depend on observed intended-user behavior rather than personas or invented adoption.

## Steps
1. Read the challenge contract, claim register and existing user journey. Choose one primary planning/analysis audience.
2. Prepare neutral tasks: identify the central activity window, compare equivalent seasons/cutoffs, explain a quiet versus missing week and export an evidence-bearing brief.
3. Prepare consent/anonymization instructions, task completion/time/help/error/source-comprehension fields and a severity rubric. Store participant identities outside Git.
4. Define a formative target of 3-5 participants including relevant practitioners where feasible. With five, seek at least four completing the core journey with <=1 prompt; report raw counts and proxy status.
5. Require comprehension of observed/adjusted/modeled values, detections versus burned area and absence of demonstrated operational warning. Do not coach the answer into the task.
6. Hand the protocol to Sheikh for H06 recruitment/contact. No messages are sent by the agent.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Protocol can be run without leading participants; failures and uncertainty are recordable.
- [ ] No claim of participants, adoption or saved losses before actual evidence.
- [ ] H06 names what genuinely requires human contact.

## Stop and ask if
Recruitment requires contacting anyone or storing identifying information in the repository. Sheikh handles contact; anonymized evidence is sufficient.
