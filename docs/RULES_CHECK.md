## What this means

- The pre-event rule is the only finding that could disqualify the project. Everything else (AI disclosure, citing
  every source, open licences, under-18 media) is already planned in cards T01, T29 and T35.
- The FAQ also says challenge statements were released on 28 Oct and challenge **resources** on 13 Nov. Read
  literally, building a solution before 14 Nov breaks the rule, whatever the AI or licence position.
- Options for Sheikh, in order of safety: (1) ask the Local Lead / GO Team in writing (conduct or info address on the
  FAQ page) whether pre-built work may be submitted, and follow their answer; (2) disclose the pre-event work openly
  in the submission; (3) rebuild during the event. This file doesn't choose; Sheikh does.
