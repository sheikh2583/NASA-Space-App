SNPP near-real-time no longer exists (decision 17).

## Added 3 Nov 2026 after the kit review (proposed by Shammo for Sheikh's approval)
17. Sensor continuity. FIRMS stopped producing new Suomi NPP fire data at 13:00 UTC on 2 Nov 2026 (NASA Earthdata blog, 27 Oct 2026); historical SNPP data stays available. SNPP stays the reference sensor for 2012 to 2 Nov 2026, so the long record is one instrument. After the cutoff, observed values come from NOAA-20 VIIRS (same sensor, about 50 minutes apart in the same orbital plane), adjusted to SNPP terms by a ratio per admin1 region and calendar month fitted on the SNPP/NOAA-20 overlap years (the same method as baseline B1). source = adjusted_from_noaa20 for those rows. NOAA-21 is optional and never mixed into the same series without its own ratio. The current-status card runs on NOAA-20 near-real-time. Terra and Aqua (MODIS) begin shutting down in late 2026 / early 2027; MODIS is a historical input only and nothing current depends on it.
18. Sensor availability. A missing grid_daily row means zero detections only on days the satellite was operating. data/processed/sensor_days.parquet (date, sat, operating, note) records which satellites were operating on each day, built from the first and last dates in the data and the FIRMS/NASA outage notices. Days a sensor was not operating are missing, never zero: they get no observed value, are excluded from climatology, and the series point gets source from the sensor that was available. Known windows: SNPP starts 20 Jan 2012 and ends 2 Nov 2026; Terra MODIS data from 20:00 UTC 26 Mar 2026 to 19:23 UTC 27 Mar 2026 are flagged questionable and excluded.
19. Per-satellite grid. grid_daily is keyed by (cell_id, date, sat) with sat in Terra | Aqua | SNPP | NOAA20 | NOAA21. Satellites are combined only in the panel (ws1). Terra flies alone from Nov 2000 to early July 2002 (Aqua's first FIRMS day); every MODIS-based estimate, B0 and B1 included, is fitted per satellite combination (terra_only, terra_aqua) so the Terra-only years are not scaled with a Terra+Aqua ratio.
20. Drawn AOIs versus region products. The API computes the series, anomaly flags, critical periods and the current-status card live for the drawn AOI, from harmonized.parquet through common/ and the ws3/ws4 functions. Drivers, drift flags and the forecast outlook are precomputed per admin1 region; for a drawn AOI the API returns those of the region that holds most of the AOI's cells and says which region in the response (region_id, region_name). The precomputed anomalies_region_week and critical_periods tables are for the static fallback and for QA.
21. Intervals on observed rows. Observed SNPP rows have lo_count = hi_count = value_count. Rows adjusted from NOAA-20 get the interval of the NOAA-20 ratio (5th and 95th percentile ratios, as in interval_table v0). Because tighter bands make the lo-above-p90 rule easier to meet, Samiul reports the flag rate per year in validation/qa.json; if the rate before 2012 is under half the rate after, the flag rule uses value (not lo) and says so in the method text.
22. Grid convention (replaces the formula in older notes): i = min(floor(round((lat + 90) * 10, 6)), 1799), j = min(floor(round((lon + 180) * 10, 6)), 3599), cell_id = i * 3600 + j. The rounding stops float error from moving points on grid lines into the wrong cell; the min keeps lat = 90 and lon = 180 on the grid. Python, DuckDB and the front end use this exact expression.
23. Day convention (replaces "Day = UTC date"): detections keep acq_date and acq_time in UTC as delivered by FIRMS. grid_daily.date is the local solar date: the UTC timestamp plus lon / 15 hours. Without this, VIIRS night passes (about 01:30 local) land on the previous day in South Asia. Weeks start on Monday of the local solar date; ISO week 53 is folded into week 52 for climatology. Responses report day_convention = "local_solar_date".
